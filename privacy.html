Privacy Policy

doodle-uploader · last updated 2 November 2026

doodle-uploader is a personal, single-user tool. Its only purpose is to let its owner upload videos they created themselves to the drafts of their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What we collect

Nothing is collected from anyone other than the owner of this tool, and nothing is collected automatically. When the owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok returns an access token and an open ID. These are stored only as a file on the owner's own computer. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API when a video is uploaded.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a server and we do not have a database.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts or location.
  • We do not read, download or store any TikTok content.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What we send to TikTok

Only the video file that the owner chooses to upload, together with the access token that authorises the upload. Videos are placed in the owner's own TikTok drafts. The owner then reviews and publishes them inside the TikTok app themselves. This tool never publishes anything on its own.

Keeping data

The access token stays on the owner's computer until it expires or is deleted. Deleting the file removes it completely. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and has no users other than its owner.
